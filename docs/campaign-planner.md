# Guided campaigns and budget advisor

The Campaigns workspace now provides a modern responsive list, a three-step creation/editing flow, and a saved campaign review. Existing campaigns retain their IDs, budgets, statuses and management actions. Search, status filtering, duplication, archiving/restoration and editing continue to use the existing authenticated APIs. Counts describe the returned records, not invented performance. Ready remains a workspace status, not proof of ad delivery.

## The client flow

1. **The brief:** campaign name, product/service, audience, target market, business outcome and platform. The selected outcome gives a relevant explanation of what to measure. Changing an existing outcome clears the old cost and target assumptions because their units may differ.
2. **Budget and ideas:** brand, currency/duration, conversion destination, website/tracking readiness, a planning target, optional cost assumption or business economics, spending ceiling, chosen content language, benefit, exact offer and video readiness. MarketFlow proposes a budget, ad copy/visual concept and relevant setup actions. The user can apply the budget or enter their own amount.
3. **Review:** the exact selected budget, brief, proposal and suggested actions appear together before saving a Draft. No ad is published and no money is charged through this flow.

Recommendations respond to actual input: define qualified enquiries, prepare a landing page or eligible native lead form, verify website conversions, prepare real creative, keep the first test focused, reconcile a funding gap, or measure awareness separately from acquisition. They do not certify account eligibility or promise results.

## Budget basis and limits

The budget engine uses one currency and never converts currencies or invents local auction prices. Enter all financial inputs in the selected currency. With a positive supplied cost, the scenario is target outcomes multiplied by that cost. Traffic uses CPC and Awareness uses CPM, so impression targets are divided by 1,000.

Without a supplied cost, Sales/Leads/Messages can use business economics:

- Contribution = revenue per closed sale × gross margin − extra variable costs, floored at zero.
- Sales allowance = 70% of contribution.
- Lead/conversation allowance = 70% of contribution × the supplied lead/message-to-sale rate.
- Scenario budget = target outcomes × allowance.

The 70% rule is a disclosed MarketFlow planning choice. An affordable allowance is not an expected cost, a platform minimum or a guarantee that the auction can deliver at that cost. Closing rates must use the same lead qualification definition as the goal. Property agents should use their own commission/revenue per closed deal, not the full property value, unless they actually receive the full value as revenue.

Defaults of 10 acquisition/traffic outcomes or 10,000 awareness impressions are editable planning targets, not performance predictions or learning-phase exit requirements. Lean/recommended/broader scenarios change the target size. The recommended total and average daily budget remain visible alongside the selected spend. A ceiling reduces selected spend and produces a funding gap; it does not silently reduce the stated target or imply the target can still be achieved.

Missing economics/close rate or CPC/CPM yields `needs_inputs` and a usable ad concept, with no invented numerical recommendation. A user can still manually plan a draft. Loss-making assumptions, non-positive contribution and unsupported scenario sizes are flagged. Non-finite/negative inputs, fractional or boolean targets, unsupported language, mixed currency labels, and sub-cent budget ceilings are rejected. Media budgets exclude production, agency fees and taxes. Provider campaign minimums and launch eligibility still require provider validation.

## Ideas, storage and privacy

Ideas are generated by the existing local content engine, in the user's chosen Egyptian Arabic, MSA or English. Awareness chooses TOFU, traffic MOFU and acquisition BOFU. The idea includes headline, hook, body, CTA and a production suggestion. Supplied facts and offer terms are the source; there is no external AI call. The same brief deterministically produces the same proposed concept, so saving a plan does not silently replace its preview. Visual suggestions do not generate images.

A nullable `campaigns.planning_json` column is added by the existing idempotent schema initialization. It stores the planning brief, calculated proposal, ad idea and whether the numerical budget was applied. Create/update recompute from validated inputs and the campaign's current facts rather than trusting client-calculated totals. A manual budget override is retained with the scenario clearly labelled as a reference. Duplication retains the plan. Old campaigns need no plan and remain usable.

Private ownership and existing CSRF/session checks apply. The preview route explicitly uses `Cache-Control: no-store`. No account secrets, `.env` changes, paid model activation or external ad writes are involved. The optional plan is saved with the existing campaign, not in a second public service.

## Strategy integration

Quick/guided strategy briefs now offer Manual or Recommend budget mode. Existing manual budgets remain authoritative. Automatic mode uses the same budget engine and replaces the missing total with the selected recommendation before existing campaign selection/eligibility checks. Results display the proposal and an optional local ad concept. Accepting a strategy stores the same proposal and concept with its Draft.

The conversational Strategy Agent keeps its existing numeric-budget path and adds a Recommend a budget button. Automatic sessions ask for the test target, optional ceiling and ad language, requesting a cost assumption when financial inputs cannot supply an allowance. Session resume/build and the earlier answer editor continue to work.
